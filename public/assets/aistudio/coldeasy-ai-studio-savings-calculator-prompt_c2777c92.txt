Add a "How much could you save?" savings calculator as one reusable component, SavingsCalculator, with a full mode and a compact mode.

WHERE IT GOES
- Home page: full mode, as its own section on sand, placed directly after "Fridge vs ColdEasy Box" and before "How ColdEasy Works".
- Product pages: compact mode, below the buy panel, with the product preselected.
- Its own route, #/calculator, in full mode. Link to it from the Science page closing CTA and from the mobile menu.

INPUTS (mobile-first, one column on phones, large controls, no free-text typing where a chip or stepper works)
1. "I am a": chips for Farmer, Vendor or retailer, FPO, Other. This only changes the wording of the result.
2. "What do you store?": chips for Tomato, Banana, Mango, Leafy greens, Onion, Flowers, Other.
3. "How much do you handle per day?": a stepper plus slider, 5 to 500 kg, default 40 kg.
4. "Average selling price": ₹ per kg, a stepper, default set per crop from mock.ts.
5. "How much do you lose to spoilage today?": a slider from 0 to 50%, default 20%, with the helper text "Your best guess is fine".
6. "Days you sell per month": a stepper, 1 to 31, default 26.
7. Product: ColdEasy Box or AutoBox – 3 Box. It is preselected on product pages and defaults to ColdEasy Box elsewhere.
Every input updates the result live. There is no "Calculate" button.

ASSUMPTIONS (in src/data/mock.ts, never hard-coded in the component)
- spoilageReduction per crop as a range { low, high }. Use placeholder values (for example low 0.3, high 0.5) and mark them PLACEHOLDER in a code comment.
- capacityKg per product (placeholder values) and the product price from the existing product data.
- runningCostPerMonth per product (water, pad care), as a placeholder value.

MATHS (show this logic exactly; keep it simple and conservative)
- kgCovered = the smaller of kg per day and (capacityKg × units). Units defaults to 1.
- lossToday per month = kg per day × spoilage % × price × days.
- savedLow and savedHigh per month = kgCovered × spoilage % × spoilageReduction.low/high × price × days, minus runningCostPerMonth. Never show a value below 0.
- paybackDays = product price ÷ (saved per month ÷ days). Show it as a range (from savedHigh to savedLow). If the payback is over 365 days, say "More than a year at this volume" rather than a number.
- If kg per day is more than one unit can hold, show a note: "One [product] holds about [capacityKg] kg. You may need [N] units." Add a stepper to change the number of units, which updates kgCovered and the price.

OUTPUT CARD (white, rounded 24px, sticky beside the inputs on desktop, below them on mobile)
- Top line, small: "Today you may be losing about ₹[lossToday] a month to spoilage."
- Hero number, large Montserrat in leaf: "You could save ₹[savedLow] – ₹[savedHigh] a month". Always show a range, never a single figure.
- Two stat tiles: "Produce saved: [kg] kg/month" and "Pays for itself in about [N]–[N] days".
- A small horizontal bar comparing "Lost today" (chilli tint) with "Lost with ColdEasy" (leaf tint). This is a simple CSS bar, not a chart library.
- Right under the hero number, a dashed PlaceholderPill: "Estimate uses placeholder reduction rates. Verified test data coming soon."
- Below it, a disclaimer in small ink-60 text: "This is an estimate, not a guarantee. Results depend on crop, weather, handling and how full the box is." Keep it visible, not hidden in a tooltip.
- A collapsible "How we calculate this" panel showing the formula in plain words and the assumption values in use.
- CTAs: a leaf pill "Buy [product]" (adds to cart with the chosen units), an outline pill "Send me this estimate on WhatsApp" (waLink with the context "Hi ColdEasy, my estimate: [crop], [kg] kg/day, saving ₹[low]–₹[high]/month. Can you help?"), and for FPO, or anyone above 150 kg/day, a text link "Get a bulk quote" that goes to #/enquiry/bulk with the crop, quantity and product prefilled.
- Share: copy a link with the inputs encoded in the URL query, so the same result opens for whoever receives it.

COMPACT MODE (product pages)
Show only crop, kg per day, price and spoilage %, plus the hero range and the payback tiles. Add a "See full breakdown →" link to #/calculator that carries the current inputs.

TRACKING
Fire track("calculator_used") once per session, on the first input change, with the crop, kg, product and the savedLow and savedHigh values. Also fire track("calculator_cta") with the type (buy, whatsapp, bulk or share) when a CTA is clicked.

COPY AND TONE
Use plain words for a second-language reader: "spoil", "save", "pays for itself". Format numbers in Indian style (₹1,25,000), round money to the nearest ₹10 and kg to whole numbers. There must be no words like "guaranteed", "assured" or "100%".

CHECK WHEN DONE
At 360px, all inputs are reachable without horizontal scroll, the result updates instantly, the output card is visible after the last input without a long scroll, and the placeholder pill and disclaimer are always visible next to the savings number.
