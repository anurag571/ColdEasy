# Legacy: Google AI Studio prototype (visual reference only)

This folder is the Vite + React prototype exported from Google AI Studio, moved here
unchanged on 24 November 2026 when the build restarted as a Next.js app at the repo root.

Rules:

- Do not import from this folder. It is excluded from lint, type checks, tests and the build.
- Use it to look at layout and interaction ideas only. Its copy, ratings, prices, impact
  figures, partner benefits and commissions are placeholders or invented. Never copy a number
  or a claim from here into the new app (see `docs/08-ai-guardrails.md` and `docs/11-kickoff-changes.md` A7).
- Media under `public/` is copied into the new app only when it is a real, approved asset.
- This folder will be deleted once Phase 2 pages are rebuilt. That deletion will be a separate,
  reviewed pull request.
