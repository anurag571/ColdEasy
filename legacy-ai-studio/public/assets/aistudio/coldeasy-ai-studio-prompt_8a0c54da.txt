Build a clickable, front-end-only prototype of the ColdEasy website. Its only job is to show the look and feel of the brand and the main page layouts. It is a visual mock, not a working store.

ABOUT COLDEASY
ColdEasy (tagline under the logo: "Product of Temperate") makes affordable post-harvest cooling products for India. The hero product, the ColdEasy Box, is a crate that keeps fruit, vegetables and flowers fresh using passive evaporative cooling (SEEC: Structurally Enhanced Evaporative Cooling). Water wets a pad, air passes through it, evaporation cools the air, and cooler air flows over the produce. It uses no electricity, compressor or refrigerant. The buyers are smallholder farmers, street vendors, retailers and FPOs (farmer producer organisations). Many of them browse on low-cost Android phones over patchy mobile data.

TECH AND SCOPE
- React + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS. Hash-based routing so every page can be linked to.
- No backend, no Gemini API calls, no real payments, no real auth. All data comes from one local mock file (src/data/mock.ts) so it is easy to replace later.
- Mobile-first. Design at 360px wide first, then scale up to tablet and desktop (max content width 1200px). No horizontal scroll at any width.
- Keep it light: system-friendly images, no video files, no heavy animation libraries. Use CSS transitions and small SVGs. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
- Pages: Home (full), Product page for the ColdEasy Box (full), Cart page plus a slide-in cart drawer (full). The nav links Products, Science, Impact, About Us and Contact go to a simple branded "Coming soon" page that has a back-to-home link.
- Cart state lives in React context so adding to cart updates the header badge on every page.

BRAND SYSTEM (follow exactly)
Colours. Define these as Tailwind theme tokens and use only these:
- ink #251A10 (deep brown-black): body text, headings, dark footer background
- sky #6FCCDD (cool blue, the snowflake in the logo): cooling and technology accents
- leaf #487537 (deep green): primary buttons, success states, check marks
- olive #9FA738 (olive green): secondary accents, tags, highlights
- marigold #F0A023 (warm orange): the sticky signup bar, sale prices, energy
- chilli #EC2727 (red): crosses in the comparison, SOLD OUT, errors. Use it sparingly.
- cream #FCFBF1: the main page background
- sand #F5F3EC: alternate section background, to separate sections
- white #FFFFFF: cards and the product page panels
Tints for subtle backgrounds and borders: sky-light #E2F5F8, leaf-light #E4EDE0, marigold-light #FFEBB6, ink-60 #5E554E (secondary text), ink-40 #A39E98 (muted text and borders).
Contrast rules: white text only on ink, leaf or chilli (and on chilli only when bold and 18px or larger). Text on sky, olive and marigold is always ink. Never set body text in sky, olive or marigold.

Typography (Google Fonts):
- Montserrat for headings, nav, prices and buttons. Weights 500, 600, 700, 800. Section headings are uppercase, weight 700, with slight tracking.
- Poppins for body copy, captions, form fields and labels. Weights 400, 500, 600.
- Scale on mobile: H1 32px, H2 24px, body 16px, captions 13px. On desktop: H1 56px, H2 36px. Line height 1.5 for body.

Logo:
- I have uploaded three PNG files: coldeasy-logo-horizontal-color.png (for light backgrounds), coldeasy-logo-horizontal-reversed-for-dark-bg.png (white wordmark, for the dark footer) and coldeasy-symbol-color.png (symbol only, for favicon and very small spaces). Use these files as they are.
- If an uploaded file cannot be loaded, show a placeholder box labelled "LOGO" at the same size. Do not redraw the logo or recreate the wordmark in a font.
- Rules from the brand book: never stretch, rotate, recolour, add shadows or outlines to, or lower the opacity of the logo. Keep clear space around it of at least the height of the letter "C" in the wordmark. Minimum height is 36px for the horizontal logo and 45px for the symbol on its own (the header can shrink to 32px when scrolled, no smaller).

Signature brand details:
- Section headings have a thin vertical accent bar on their left: a 4px wide, rounded strip split into stacked segments of sky, marigold, chilli and leaf. This comes straight from the brand book and should appear on every section heading.
- Shapes are soft. Cards and images use large rounded corners (20 to 24px), buttons are fully rounded pills, and there are no sharp boxes.
- Brand pattern: a grid of soft, rounded leaf or seed shapes (a half-rounded square, like a petal). Build it as an SVG background. Use it only at low contrast (cream on sand, or olive at about 8% opacity), and only in the hero corner, the signup modal and the "Coming soon" page. A bolder version, made of quarter circles and leaf shapes in sky, marigold, chilli, olive, leaf and pale yellow, can appear as a small decorative tile (about 120px) on the testimonials card corner. Never place patterns behind a product image.
- Imagery: warm, natural daylight photography of Indian farmers and vendors with fresh produce, and clean product shots on soft, plain backgrounds. For this prototype, use labelled placeholder blocks (rounded, sand-coloured, with a small icon and text such as "PRODUCT IMAGE: ColdEasy Box, front view") rather than stock photos.
- Tone of voice: warm, simple, confident and honest. Use short sentences that a farmer reading in their second language can follow. Do not use hype words like "revolutionary" or "world's best".
